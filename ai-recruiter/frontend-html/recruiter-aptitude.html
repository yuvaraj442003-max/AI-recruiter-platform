📋 How It Works

  • 1. Select Topics: Pick from high-yield aptitude topics or add company-specific syllabus areas.
  • 2. AI Synthesis: Gemini generates 4 distinct options, exactly 1 mathematically verified answer, and step-by-step reasoning.
  • 3. Question Studio: Review every question, edit options or text, regenerate unwanted items, or delete questions.
  • 4. Approve & Publish: The test is saved in draft mode first. Only approved tests are released to candidates.
