🗺️ The 5-Phase Roadmap: From School to College to Career

Follow this clear roadmap to avoid common traps and secure admissions with zero stress.

1
Phase 1: Stream Alignment & Board Mastery (Class 10 & 12)

Master foundational NCERT concepts. Maintain consistent mock scores in Physics, Chemistry, Maths/Biology or Accountancy. For 10th students, choose Science, Commerce, or Polytechnic based on whether you prefer theory or hands-on technical skills.

Target: >80% in Boards
2
Phase 2: Entrance Exam Registration & Mock Tests (Oct – March)

Apply for JEE Main (Nov), NEET-UG (Feb), CUET-UG (Feb), CLAT (July), and NATA. Keep your 10th marksheet, Aadhaar, and Community Certificate scanned and ready. Solve at least 15 timed previous-year question papers (PYQs).

Action: Track Deadlines in Calendar
3
Phase 3: Results & Cutoff Calculation (April – May)

Calculate your state cutoff marks (e.g., TNEA Cutoff out of 200 = Maths + Physics/2 + Chemistry/2). Check rank lists and benchmark your percentile against previous years' opening and closing ranks.

Benchmarking: Safe, Target & Dream Colleges
4
Phase 4: Centralized Online Counseling & Choice Filling (May – July)

Participate in JoSAA (IITs/NITs), TNEA (Anna University), MCC (Medical AIQ), or DOTE (Polytechnic). Fill choices smartly in priority order: start with Dream colleges, then Target, and keep 10+ Safe options to guarantee a seat.

Key Step: Smart Choice Locking
5
Phase 5: College Reporting, Scholarship Claim & Career Launch (Aug)

Report to your allotted college with original certificates. Immediately apply for National Scholarship Portal (NSP), First Generation Graduate Concession, or 7.5% Govt School Free Quota to avoid financial burden.

Final Goal: Graduation & High-Paying Placement
